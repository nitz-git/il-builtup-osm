# Golden probes

`golden-probes.csv` is generated by `scripts/make_probes.py` (deterministic;
CI fails if the committed file differs from a regeneration). It contains
public coordinates of places and roads only. No user data, no drive tracks.

| Group | Seeds | Expected | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `loc_*` | CBS locality reference points, stratified (cities, towns, Arab urban, Negev Bedouin, Arab rural, moshav, kibbutz, community, other rural, West Bank) | urban | Tests coverage of the footprint under the UC-Q1 rule; not a legal (sign 424/425) label |
| `open_country` | Midpoints between rural localities 6-12 km apart, far from all localities; snapped in CI to the nearest trunk/primary/secondary/tertiary road | non_urban | Rejected if the snapped point is < 2.5 km from a CBS locality point |
| `corridor_*` | Hand-placed seeds on Ayalon (20), Routes 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and the Carmel Tunnels; snapped to the matching OSM way | non_urban | Route 4 rows are `medium` confidence [verify signage] |
| `urban_arterial_*` | Begin Blvd (50), Namir Rd | urban | Hard cases: fast urban arterials must stay urban |

Columns: `id, group, expected, confidence, lat, lon, snap, snap_radius_m,
min_loc_dist_m, note`. `snap` = conditions joined by `&`, each `key~regex`.

Scoring (`scripts/run_probes.py`): probes within 30 m of a polygon edge, and
probes that could not be resolved, are excluded and listed. Pass = at least 95%
correct (UC-Q9).

Attribution: locality points from the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS)
locality file 2024; road geometry used for snapping © OpenStreetMap
contributors (ODbL 1.0), so resolved probe files are ODbL-derived.
